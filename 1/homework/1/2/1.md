## 复杂度

1.  $f_1(n) = \log(\log(n)) \Longrightarrow O(\log(\log n))$

2.  $f_2(n) = 2^{\log_2 n} \Longrightarrow  O(n)$

3.  $f_3(n) = n^8\Longrightarrow O(n^8)$

4.  $f_4(n) = n^8 + n!\Longrightarrow O(n!)$

5.  $f_5(n) = n^{16} + n \log n\Longrightarrow O(n^{16})$

6.  $f_6(n) = e^n + e^{16}\Longrightarrow O(e^n)$

## 排序

$$f_4(n) > f_6(n) > f_5(n) > f_3(n) > f_2(n) > f_1(n)$$

or
$$O(n!) > O(e^n) > O(n^{16}) > O(n^8) > O(n) > O(\log(\log n))$$
